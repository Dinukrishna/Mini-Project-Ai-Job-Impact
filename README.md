# 🤖 AI Impact on Jobs & Employee Productivity

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on jobs, workforce trends, employee productivity, salary changes, and automation risks across different industries.

The project explores how AI adoption is transforming job roles, influencing employee performance, and changing workforce requirements.

Using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**, this project demonstrates an end-to-end workforce analytics workflow — from data cleaning and preprocessing to calculated measures, interactive dashboards, and business insights.

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

- **Dataset:** AI Job Impact Dataset
- **Total Records:** 2,000+
- **Total Features:** 17 columns
- **Data Types:** Numerical and Categorical
- **Domain:** Human Resources (HR) & Workforce Analytics

- Employee ID

- Job Role

## 🔄 Data Preparation & Cleaning (Excel)

Data preprocessing and cleaning were performed using Microsoft Excel.

Key activities included:

- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing values.
- Standardized text and numerical formats.
- Applied filters and sorting for data organization.
- Created calculated columns for additional analysis.
- Generated Pivot Tables to summarize workforce data.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for Power BI visualization.

### Feature Engineering

Created additional calculated columns:

- **Salary Change** – Difference between salary before and after AI adoption.
- **Salary Change %** – Percentage change in employee salary.
- **Experience Level** – Categorization based on employee experience.
- **AI Impact Category** – Classification of AI's impact on job roles.

## 🧠 Key Business Insights

Based on the analysis:

1. **AI Adoption Across Industries:** Technology and Finance industries demonstrate the highest levels of AI adoption.

2. **Remote Work Productivity:** Remote employees demonstrate higher productivity improvement compared to other work arrangements.

3. **Job Transformation:** AI impacts jobs more through modification and transformation rather than complete replacement.

4. **Automation Risk:** Repetitive job roles face higher automation risks due to AI-driven automation.

5. **Salary Trends:** Experienced employees receive higher salaries after AI adoption.

6. **Employee Upskilling:** Upskilling and reskilling are essential for employees working in industries with higher automation risks.

7. **Workforce Transformation:** AI adoption is changing traditional job responsibilities and creating new workforce requirements.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Invest in Employee Upskilling:** Organizations should provide continuous AI training and skill development programs.

2. **Prepare for Automation Risks:** Identify repetitive job roles and develop workforce transition strategies.

3. **Promote AI Adoption:** Encourage the effective use of AI tools to improve employee productivity and operational efficiency.

4. **Focus on Employee Productivity:** Evaluate how AI adoption influences employee performance across different work arrangements.

5. **Support Experienced Employees:** Leverage experienced employees to guide AI implementation and workforce transformation.

6. **Monitor Salary Changes:** Regularly evaluate salary trends before and after AI adoption.

7. **Improve Workforce Planning:** Use AI adoption and automation risk insights to support long-term HR strategies.

8. **Maintain Employee Satisfaction:** Monitor job satisfaction during AI-driven organizational changes.
